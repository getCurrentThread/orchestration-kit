# Handoff

```
Current Role: Maintenance

```

Current Role is one of: Architecture, Research, Reference Implementation, Feature Implementation,
Release / Operations, Incident Response, Maintenance.

## Current Objective

The one thing the owner asked for, in their terms.

## Active/Open Tracks

One line per track: what it is, its Orca task or dispatch id, its state, and the next step.

## Accepted Artifacts

Work that was verified and accepted: paths, commits, and how each was checked.

## Deferred Work

What was postponed and why.

## Open Questions

Questions waiting on the owner, and decisions that must not be reopened without them.

## Recommended Next Role

The role the successor should take first, and why.

## Observed Baseline

Facts measured at handoff time: branch and commit, test results with their output, the Orca Run id,
and workers still running.
